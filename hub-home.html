Home Assistant Family Hub Calendar Sync

This is a small personal application that connects my own Google Calendar to my private Home Assistant smart-home system so that my family's schedule can be shown on a wall-mounted display in my home.

What it does

The application reads calendar events from my Google account and displays them on a family dashboard. It is used only by me and members of my household. It is not a public service and is not offered to other users.
